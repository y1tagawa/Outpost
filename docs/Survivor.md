# Survivor

A Companion for the First Night  
Concept White Paper Version 0.1

## Abstract

Survivor is not a survival kit.  
It is a learning companion designed to remain useful when normal society is no longer available.

During everyday life, it encourages children to observe nature, ask questions, and record discoveries.  
During disasters, it helps them remain calm, navigate toward safety, and survive the first night.

Survivor does not replace parents, teachers, or communities.  
Instead, it preserves the child's ability to continue learning and acting independently when those support systems become temporarily unavailable.

## Vision

Every child should have the opportunity to explore the world.  
Every child should also have the opportunity to survive it.

Learning and survival are not separate activities.  
The ability to recognize a plant may become the ability to identify food.  
The ability to understand geography may become the ability to find a safe route.  
The habit of keeping a field journal may become the habit of recording vital information during an emergency.

Survivor exists to preserve both curiosity and resilience.
